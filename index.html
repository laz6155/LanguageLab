GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions,
answered.

Is LanguageLab already open for enrollment?

Not yet. We are collecting early interest while shaping programs and the academy platform. Opening dates will be confirmed later.

Can I join online?

Online learning is part of the planned model. Available formats and schedules will be announced when each program opens.

Can I use the educator platform now?

Yes. The educator and student classroom tools are accessible. Teacher accounts and class data are protected by sign-in and permissions; further improvements are underway.

Where does Eryaman Speaking Club fit into the academy?

Eryaman Speaking Club is the speaking-practice and community program within LanguageLab Akademi. You can explore both learning programs and speaking meetups in one academy.
