GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions,
answered.

Is LanguageLab already open for enrollment?

Not yet. We are collecting early interest while shaping programs and the academy platform. Opening dates will be confirmed later.

Can I join online?

Online learning is part of the planned model. Available formats and schedules will be announced when each program opens.

Is the educator platform available now?

The LanguageLab educator workspace is currently in development. Educators can contact us to share their needs and explore collaboration.

Is LanguageLab the same as Eryaman Speaking Club?

No. LanguageLab is a separate academy project being developed from the same community-building experience. Eryaman Speaking Club activities continue on their own website.
